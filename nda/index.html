Kendra Participant Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is between Kendra Skilling ("Kendra") and you, the person taking the Kendra assessment ("you").

1. Purpose

You are taking part in the Kendra pilot, which may include taking the assessment, receiving results and reports, and sharing feedback on Kendra's products, services, and ideas (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, Kendra will share non-public information with you, and you may share information with Kendra.

2. What is Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information about Kendra that you see or receive, in any form, including: assessment questions, scenarios and answer options; scoring methods and the skills framework; reports, report formats and results templates; product designs, features and prototypes; research, data, algorithms and software; business plans, strategies, pricing and customer information; and any other materials related to Kendra's concept or operations.

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) was publicly known at the time of disclosure; (b) becomes publicly known through no fault of yours; (c) you already had without breaking any obligation; (d) you develop independently without using Confidential Information; or (e) you lawfully receive from a third party who is free to share it.

3. Your commitments

You agree to: (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) not share, copy, screenshot, record, post or distribute it to anyone without Kendra's prior written consent; (c) take reasonable care to protect it from unauthorized access or disclosure; (d) not reverse-engineer, deconstruct or try to replicate any product, concept or idea contained in it; and (e) delete or return it when Kendra asks.

4. No license or ownership

Nothing in this Agreement gives you any rights, ownership or license to Kendra's Confidential Information or intellectual property. All rights remain with Kendra. Kendra may freely use any feedback or suggestions you give, without owing you anything.

5. No obligation to proceed

This Agreement does not obligate either side to continue any business relationship or transaction.

6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Israel.

7. Entire agreement

This Agreement is the entire understanding between you and Kendra regarding confidentiality and replaces any earlier agreements or discussions on this subject.

8. Approval

By ticking the box "I have read and approve the Kendra NDA" in the app, you confirm that you have read this Agreement, agree to its terms, and that this approval has the same effect as your signature. Kendra records the date and time of your approval.
